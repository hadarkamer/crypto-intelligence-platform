# Active plan: formula discovery and validation only

Reaffirmed 2026-10-10. This active scope supersedes the temporary restoration of
trading and automatic-worker objectives at 21:17 UTC that day, as well as the
older roadmap's activation stages. The deliverable is a complete formula-and-
evidence report: discovery, fixed prospective validation and documented limits.
Trade lifecycle development, alert/exchange activation and development or
deployment of a new automatic worker are outside the active work.
Retain the existing source collector and existing parallel Hyperliquid Testnet
bot as independent systems. This planning change does not stop or reconfigure
them, their existing workers, runtime flags or scientific policies.

## Report and stop conditions

Report exact formula conditions and versions, symbol, base and analysis
directions, threshold, each window's resolved-parent count, successes, losses,
OPEN/AMBIGUOUS/missing evidence, hit rate and Wilson lower bound. Preserve
occurrence/parent evidence, exclusions, coverage limitations, selection status
and prospective qualification separately. Retain the full source and result
bindings; a summary of percentages is not the evidence package.

The discovery phase ends when every original request has a documented terminal
result or explicit acquisition block and all possible complete selections have
been reported. A blocked request prevents its complete selection and stays
visible; it is not a fabricated empty window or a successful research result.
Operational failures can resume the exact request after correction, without
changing its population or cutoff.

A completed selection with no candidates ends that plan with no nominations.
For nominated scopes, the fixed validation ends with a complete report that
may contain zero qualified formulas. Unfinished evidence remains INCOMPLETE;
it cannot receive final qualification. Do not extend a cutoff, lower a gate,
drop a failed scope or open another window merely to obtain a pass. Any later
experiment is a separate explicit research decision.

Rank, selection and a fresh gate pass are different findings. The native path
does not correct for multiple hypothesis testing; its ranking remains exposed
to selection and overfitting risk. BTC-parent grouping does not prove
statistical independence, and qualification does not establish profitability.
Report those limits without adding new acceptance thresholds. Repeat software
tests only to investigate a concrete defect or a changed implementation.
